# MIDTERM REASONING ENGINE V1

Status: FROZEN

---

# Purpose

MidTerm Reasoning Engine is the intelligence layer of SmartTahlil.

Its responsibility is NOT to calculate indicators.

Its responsibility is NOT to detect market structures.

Those responsibilities belong to Engine1, Engine2 and future engines.

MidTerm receives validated findings from all analysis engines and transforms them into defendable market knowledge.

---

# Philosophy

SmartTahlil separates knowledge generation into independent layers.

Raw Data

↓

Observation

↓

Finding

↓

Claim

↓

Insight

↓

Narrative

---

# Layer Definitions

## 1. Observation

Raw market data.

Examples:

OHLC

Volume

Prices

No interpretation exists.

---

## 2. Finding

Generated by analysis engines.

Examples:

Bullish BOS detected

EMA20 > EMA50

RSI Overbought

Liquidity Sweep

Order Block Found

A Finding is a verified analytical discovery directly extracted from market data.

No strategic reasoning exists yet.

---

## 3. Claim

Generated ONLY by MidTerm.

A Claim is a defendable market statement supported by one or more Findings.

Examples:

Short-term trend is bullish.

Market structure has shifted.

Liquidity has likely been collected.

Each Claim must contain:

Result

Supporting Findings

Confidence

---

## 4. Insight

Generated by MidTerm.

Insight combines multiple Claims into higher-level understanding.

Examples:

High probability bullish continuation.

Strong reversal conditions forming.

Institutional buying pressure confirmed.

Insight represents strategic understanding.

---

## 5. Narrative

Generated after MidTerm.

Narrative NEVER creates knowledge.

Narrative only explains existing knowledge in human language.

Examples:

Persian

English

Arabic

Turkish

Changing language must never change analysis.

---

# MidTerm Responsibilities

Receive Findings

Validate relationships

Generate Claims

Calculate confidence

Generate Insights

Prepare structured knowledge

Return MidTerm Output

MidTerm never performs technical calculations.

---

# Confidence

Confidence belongs to Claims.

Each Claim contains its own confidence score.

Example:

Trend is bullish

Confidence:

92%

Confidence is calculated from supporting Findings.

---

# Human Decision Principle

SmartTahlil never decides for the trader.

SmartTahlil produces knowledge.

The trader makes the final decision.

---

# Golden Rules

Rule 1

Engines discover.

MidTerm reasons.

Narrative communicates.

---

Rule 2

Findings never perform reasoning.

---

Rule 3

Claims must always be supported by Findings.

---

Rule 4

Insights are built from Claims.

---

Rule 5

Narrative never changes knowledge.

Only presentation changes.

---

Rule 6

Knowledge has no language.

Only its expression has language.

---

Rule 7

SmartTahlil equips traders to make better decisions.

It never makes decisions for them.
